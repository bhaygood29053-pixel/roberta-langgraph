## Source identity

The Guiding and Establishing National Innovation for U.S. Stablecoins Act
(GENIUS Act) is Public Law 119-27, approved July 18, 2025.

Canonical law source:
https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/PLAW-119publ27

freshness-sensitive regulatory source. Treasury implementation material is

## Effective-date rule

The statute uses an earlier-of structure: January 18, 2027, or 120 days after
the primary federal payment stablecoin regulators issue final implementing
regulations.

ROBERTA must not turn a proposed rule into a final rule, or a final rule into
an effective current obligation without current evidence.

## Authority boundary

This capsule is static learning material. It may teach statutory concepts and
source identity. It does not prove current:

- issuer licensing or permission status;
- stablecoin compliance;
- reserve composition;
- redemption availability;
- sanctions/AML compliance;
- token identity;
- bridge identity;
- custody arrangement;
- liquidity;
- current rulemaking status.

Those changing facts require current accepted CMIS regulatory evidence.

## Native versus bridged rule

A bridged or wrapped representation must remain distinct from its underlying
stablecoin. Evidence about underlying USDC cannot by itself establish that a
USDC.X representation has the same bridge, custody, redemption, liquidity, or
regulatory risk.

## Prohibited inference

This source does not authorize ROBERTA to output COMPLIANT or NON_COMPLIANT.
ROBERTA may explain applicable statutory concepts and may report a bounded
CMIS applicability state, but legal-compliance conclusions require a separate
